Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version: your entries belong to you and are visible only to your account. They are sent to an AI model only when you use the guide. There are no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sold. You can download or delete your entries at any time.

Who runs Second Thought

Second Thought is a small personal project run by Elissa Esterlein, an individual, not a company. Questions or requests: elissa.esterlein@gmail.com.

What is collected

  • Your account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash; nobody can read them.
  • Your journal: what you write, the guide's questions and replies, summaries, intentions and check-ins, and the dates of each entry.
  • A daily usage count: how many AI requests your account made today, used only to enforce the daily limit. It does not contain what you wrote.
  • Standard technical logs: the hosting providers below keep routine logs, such as IP address and browser type, to run and protect their services.

Second Thought has no ads, no analytics or tracking cookies, and does not sell or share your information for marketing.

How the AI guide uses your writing

When you use a guided feature (a reflection, writing questions, a summary, or Look back), the text needed for that request is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the reply. Depending on the feature, that can include the current conversation and some recent entries or intentions, so the guide can follow up on them. Anthropic processes this as a service provider. If you write without the guide, nothing is sent to an AI model.

Who can see your entries

Only your account can read your entries; this is enforced by the database's row-level security rules. Your journal is not end-to-end encrypted. As the operator, I have administrative access to the database. I do not read entries, except when you ask me to help fix a problem with your account or when the law requires it.

Where your data is stored

  • Supabase hosts accounts and entries in the United States (Oregon region).
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's web pages. It does not receive your entries.
  • Anthropic receives the text of guided requests, as described above.

Keeping, exporting and deleting

  • Your entries are kept until you delete them.
  • You can delete any entry from your notebook at any time.
  • You can download a full copy of your journal from Settings.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, email elissa.esterlein@gmail.com from your account's email address. Your account, entries, and usage records are deleted within 30 days. Routine backups held by the database host expire on their normal schedule.

Sensitive information

Journals often hold personal feelings and health-related thoughts. Second Thought uses what you write only to provide the journal and guide to you. It is not a healthcare provider and is not covered by HIPAA.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, each account can reach only its own entries, and AI use is rate limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, you will be notified by email.

Age

Second Thought is for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and accounts known to belong to children will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be emailed to account holders.
